Questions

Before you book.

Is the price I see the price I pay?
Yes, for the home you described. If the home is materially different from what was entered — more rooms, or a level of build-up that needs a deep clean rather than a maintenance visit — we tell you before we start, never after we finish. And if it needs less than you booked, we charge less.
Do I have to be home?
No. Most of our recurring customers are at work. Leave a code, a lockbox or a key and tell us in the notes. The crew locks up and texts you when they are out.
Do you bring your own supplies?
Everything, including the vacuum, and the standard line is eco-friendly and non-toxic — safe around children and pets. If you prefer we use a specific product on a particular surface — stone, hardwood, a finish you are protective of — leave it out with a note and we will use it.
What if something is missed?
Call or text within 48 hours and we come back and clean it. No argument and no charge. That is the whole guarantee, and it is the reason to hire a company with an address rather than a stranger with a bucket.
Are you insured, and are the cleaners checked?
Licensed, bonded and insured, and accredited by the Better Business Bureau with an A+ rating since 2020. Cleaners are interviewed in person and background-checked before they are ever sent to a home.
Am I locked into a contract?
No. Recurring pricing is a discount for keeping the schedule, not a contract. Skip a visit, change the frequency, or stop entirely — there is no fee and no notice period.
Why is the first recurring visit more expensive?
Because it is a different job. The first visit is a full reset that catches up everything a maintenance clean assumes was already done. From the second visit on you are at the recurring rate, and it stays there.
Do you clean rentals between guests?
Yes, and it is one of the fastest-growing parts of the business at the Beaches. Give us the calendar and we work the turns, launder and restock the linens, and photograph anything a guest damaged so you have it in time to file.
